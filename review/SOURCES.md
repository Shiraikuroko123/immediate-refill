# Artifact sources

The frozen scientific files were copied by SHA-256 from the author snapshot with digest `a01fa19a146623991592a72fcf47770730ec9a4bef52da4d93b05fd7d80e6d12`. The CPU scripts and the portable GPU setup are supplemental reproduction tools. Historical event timestamps, model fingerprints and invocation paths in the raw records describe the original experiment. Programs in this supplement take their run paths from the extracted files or command-line arguments.

The GSM8K and AMC reasoning trajectories are from Sophie L. Wang, Amil Dravid, Rulin Shao, Kevin Farhat, Sewon Min and Alexei A. Efros, *Base Models Can Reason by Taking a Cue From Training Data*. The data card identifies CC BY 4.0. The original shard bytes and identifiers used by the replay are included alongside derived counts, scheduling simulations and timing estimates. The transformation fixes path order and interprets returned votes and token lengths as specified in the paper. Other input cohorts and their evaluation protocols are identified in the manuscript and the associated frozen records. Derived BRUMO quantities use Wenbo Chen and collaborators' MARS project, revision `9b567272ac917175d377c921291356510d90f8cb`, under its dataset card's CC BY 4.0 terms. The PDF uses the ICML 2027 author template. The .sty and .bst files retain the notices supplied with the styles. Model weights are restored from the external repositories under the corresponding repository terms.
